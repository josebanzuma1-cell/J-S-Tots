Finding us

Look for the church — we are straight across from it.

J & S Tots Pre-School sits directly opposite Munyonyo C.O.U. Church in Munyonyo, Kampala. If you can find the church, you have found us.

Coming by boda or taxi, ask for Munyonyo C.O.U. Church and get down there. Driving, there is somewhere to pull in at drop-off and pick-up time.

If you get turned around, call 0774 197 708 and we will talk you in.
