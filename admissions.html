How to enrol

Four steps, and your child has a place.

  • 01

    Get in touch

    Call 0774 197 708, send us a WhatsApp message, or fill in the form further down this page. Tell us your child's age and the class you have in mind.

  • 02

    Come and visit

    Bring your child if you can. See the classrooms, meet the teachers, watch how the day runs and ask whatever you want to ask. We would rather you came and looked than took our word for it.

  • 03

    Register

    We complete the admission form together, agree the right class for your child, and confirm fees, uniform and what to bring. You will leave knowing exactly what happens next.

  • 04

    First day

    We plan the first day carefully, especially for our youngest. Some children settle in an hour, some take a fortnight — both are normal, and we will keep you posted either way.

Before you come

What to bring to registration.

Nothing complicated. If you are missing something, come anyway and we will sort it out.

  • Your child's birth certificate or an age record
  • Immunisation card, where you have one
  • A recent passport photo of your child
  • Any report or record from a previous school
  • Your own contact details and those of one other guardian

Fees, uniform and term dates are confirmed at registration, as they change from term to term. Please call for the current figures rather than relying on an old list.
